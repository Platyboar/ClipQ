Privacy & Data Storage

When hosted online, your settings, clip memory, and clip history are stored in the EU (Cloudflare D1) linked only to your Twitch User ID. No Twitch access tokens are stored on the server. Playing clips loads external embeds from Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram. You can export or delete your stored data anytime under Settings → General.
